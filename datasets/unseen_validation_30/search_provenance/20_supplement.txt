## What is Aquaphor® made of?

All Aquaphor products are made to care for skin using dermatologically-tested formulas. Aquaphor Healing Ointment® is formulated to soothe and nourish skin with 41% Petrolatum (skin protectant) and additional ingredients Panthenol, Glycerin, Mineral Oil, Ceresin, Bisabolol, and Lanolin Alcohol. ## How does Aquaphor® work?

Aquaphor Healing Ointment® helps to protect skin by forming a protective barrier to help skin to retain moisture. Aquaphor Healing Ointment®’s formula offers effective moisturizing properties that help attract and retain water in the skin. By creating a semi-occlusive barrier on the skin that allows the outflow of excess fluid and the inflow of oxygen, it allows skin to ‘breathe’ and helps to create an ideal healing environment for the skin.

Frequently Asked Questions

## How often should I apply Aquaphor Healing Ointment®?

You can apply Aquaphor Healing Ointment® as often as you like to soothe and smooth areas of dry or irritated skin. The handy 0.25oz mini-jar and the 1.75oz tube make it easy to pop in your bag and carry around with you wherever you go. 

## Can I use Aquaphor Healing Ointment® to care for wounds?

Aquaphor Healing Ointment® can be used on minor wounds like cuts and scrapes, but the product should not be applied to open, bleeding or wet wounds. If you are in any way concerned about the time a wound is taking to heal, or think it may have become infected, consult your doctor for advice. 

## Is Aquaphor Healing Ointment® suitable for use on my baby’s skin?

Aquaphor® Baby Healing Ointment or Aquaphor Healing Ointment® is a gentle formula suitable for baby’s skin care needs. This multi-purpose ointment can be used for many of your baby’s skin needs, from chapped cheeks to minor scrapes and scratches, diaper rash, dry skin, and many more. You can find out more about caring for your baby’s skin in the article Soothing Baby’s Dry Skin. 

## Can I use Aquaphor Healing Ointment® to care for skin with a minor burn?

Yes. Aquaphor Healing Ointment® is ideal for use on superficial, minor burns and small areas of skin. Do not use the ointment on more severe burns or any burns that are bleeding. You can read more about caring for minor burns here. 

## Is Aquaphor Healing Ointment® suitable for use on very dry skin all over my face and body?

Yes. Aquaphor Healing Ointment® can be used anywhere externally on your face and body to soothe dry skin. It is particularly well-suited to commonly dry areas of skin such as chapped lips, dry, cracked hands or feet.
